## Unlimited 4x4 - Public Class Designation

"Unlimited 4x4" and its common derivatives (including but not limited to 
"U4x4," "Unlimited 4X4," "U4X4") are proposed as public, open class 
designations for monster truck racing.

These terms are intentionally released into the public domain for use 
by any promoter, team, or organization. No entity should claim exclusive 
trademark rights to these terms as applied to monster truck/extreme 4x4 
racing events.

Common usage examples:
- Unlimited 4x4 Championship
- U4x4 Series
- Unlimited 4x4 Racing
- Unlimited 4X4
